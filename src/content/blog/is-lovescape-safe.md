<p class="article-lede">Lovescape safety depends on several layers: who appears in the content, whether source media is authorized, what you reveal in chat, whether a character is public, how generated files are stored, what Chips cost, how subscriptions renew, and whether deletion controls cover every asset.</p>

## Is Lovescape safe to use?

No online adult companion or generator is risk-free. A hosted service may process account data, prompts, chats, and media to operate its features. Accounts can be compromised, product rules can change, and users can accidentally publish or retain more than intended.

Lovescape publishes help-center guidance, community rules, and a moderation policy. Those are positive signals, not a guarantee. Users should still minimize sensitive input and verify current policies themselves.

## Use fictional adults only

Lovescape is for adults 18 and older and states that characters and content are fictional. Its moderation policy prohibits minors, non-consensual scenarios, unauthorized real-person depictions, and deepfakes.

Create a clearly adult fictional identity. Avoid youth-coded descriptions, ambiguous ages, school settings, or any scenario involving a minor. Do not use a celebrity, former partner, coworker, private person, or social-media photo as an adult character without documented permission.

## Upload only authorized media

Image-to-video and editing tools can make a still appear more lifelike. That increases the importance of consent.

Use only:

- images you generated from an original fictional prompt;
- your own image when the service explicitly permits it;
- media with clear rights and explicit permission for this transformation.

Public availability is not permission. A photo on a social profile, website, or search engine still depicts a real person with rights.

<div class="article-note"><strong>Hard boundary:</strong> never create deceptive intimate media of a real person and never use anyone under 18 or age-ambiguous in adult content.</div>

## Protect account and chat privacy

Use a unique password and secure the connected email account. Enable multi-factor authentication if offered. Avoid shared devices and saved sessions.

Keep legal names, addresses, employer details, phone numbers, finances, health records, identity documents, passwords, and other people's secrets out of chat and prompts. Fictional substitutions are enough for roleplay and reduce the impact of a leak or account error.

Do not assume “private chat” means end-to-end encrypted or inaccessible to the provider. Read the current privacy policy for processing, retention, service providers, legal disclosure, and deletion.

## Check character visibility

Lovescape allows community discovery and character sharing. Character profiles may have public visibility, so inspect the setting before saving a detailed profile.

Use invented biographies and avoid uploading a real person's face or voice. Before sharing, review the public title, avatar, description, tags, sample chat, and any generated media that may reveal information.

## Understand clear chat versus delete character

Lovescape's character-profile guide documents separate actions:

- Clear Chat deletes chat history while keeping the character.
- Delete Character permanently removes the character and cannot be undone.

Those actions may not automatically cover every downloaded file, shared link, generated image, video, voice item, payment record, backup, or account-level record. Inspect each layer and review the current privacy policy for retention details.

Keep your own deletion checklist: chat, profile, public link, image, video, voice, downloads, subscription, and account.

## Chips and subscription safety

Lovescape Premium renews automatically according to official help-center guidance. Cancellation is available through account settings, with access continuing until the billing period ends.

Purchased Chips are documented as non-expiring and non-refundable. Only active subscribers can buy them, and costs should appear before generation. Before paying:

1. record the renewal date and full price;
2. inspect included Chips and media limits;
3. confirm whether failed generations spend currency;
4. check what happens to Chips after cancellation;
5. save cancellation confirmation and review statements.

Marketing discounts can emphasize the first term rather than renewal. Evaluate the total price you expect to pay for six or twelve months.

## Moderation and community risk

Lovescape describes a hybrid system using automated detection and human review, plus reporting and appeals. Moderation can reduce obvious abuse but cannot prevent every problematic prompt, public character, or generated output.

Report characters that depict minors, impersonate real people, expose personal data, encourage coercion, or otherwise violate rules. Do not download or redistribute questionable content.

## Emotional and time boundaries

AI companions simulate attention and consistency; they are not people and do not have human feelings, consent, commitment, or professional judgment. Set a session limit and protect sleep, work, finances, and offline relationships.

Do not use an AI companion as emergency support, medical advice, therapy, legal counsel, or a financial adviser. Contact a qualified person or emergency service when real-world safety is involved.

## Five-minute safer setup

1. Create a unique password and fictional persona.
2. Confirm every character is clearly adult and fictional.
3. Locate visibility, chat, character, media, and account deletion controls.
4. Record renewal terms and set a monthly Chip budget.
5. Upload only authorized media and keep private information out.

Repeat the review after major feature or policy changes.

## Verdict

Lovescape publishes meaningful boundaries around adults, consent, real-person likenesses, moderation, and community behavior. Users still carry responsibility for account security, input sensitivity, character visibility, media rights, spending, and emotional balance. If you cannot find or accept the current privacy, deletion, or billing terms, do not upload sensitive media or pay.
