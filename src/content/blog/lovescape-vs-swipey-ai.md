<p class="article-lede">Both services offer companion chat, character creation, and generated media, but the route into the experience differs. Lovescape starts with shaping a fictional character and refining their interactions and images. Swipey AI also lets you build a private companion, but its Explorer feed, relationship progression, and verified-creator category introduce choices that a simple “features” checklist misses.</p>

This comparison is based on the providers' linked documentation, not a claim that we bought both plans or measured output quality. Feature availability and checkout terms can change. Use the same adult, fictional scenario to test the experience you would actually repeat.

## Lovescape vs Swipey AI at a glance

| Decision point | Lovescape | Swipey AI |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Starting point | Build or discover a fictional companion, then chat and generate character images | Browse the Explorer feed or create a Custom AI companion |
| Character provenance | Provider describes fictional adult characters and prohibits real-person replicas | Platform Models, Custom AI, and Verified Creator Models are different categories; the last involve real, verified creators |
| Media and voice | Images are described; video is still presented as an evolving feature | Photos, videos, and voice calls are described in the current FAQ |
| Progression | Character identity and continuity are central | Each chat has its own relationship level; some features depend on Premium or a creator Fan Club |
| Extra spend | Chips, mainly for image generation; only active subscribers can buy them | Hearts for extras; Premium and creator Fan Clubs are separate access decisions |

## Decide whether to design or discover

Lovescape's [platform overview](https://help.lovescape.com/hc/en-us/articles/26017197133713-What-Is-Lovescape-AI-Companions-Chat-Image-Video-Generation-Platform) describes control over a character's personality, appearance, interaction tone, chat, and generated images. It also mentions discovering characters made by others. A fair reason to choose it is therefore not “it has creation and Swipey does not”; Swipey has creation too. The difference to test is how directly you can turn one character brief into a stable conversation and a set of matching visuals.

Swipey's [getting-started page](https://swipey.ai/faq/getting-started/how-swipey-ai-works) foregrounds the Explorer feed: scroll through images, reels, and companions, then chat or create your own. That may be quicker for someone who likes finding a ready-made character before writing a detailed profile. For a custom character, however, its [model-types guide](https://swipey.ai/faq/ai-features/platform-models-vs-verified-creator-models-vs-custom-ai) says chatting with your own Custom AI requires Premium. Verify that step in the current checkout rather than assuming every part of “free to create” is free to use.

## Check who or what the character represents

Swipey separates Platform Models made by the service, private Custom AI built by a user, and Verified Creator Models that pair a real creator's content with an AI persona. The creator route is an intentional product category, not a license for users to upload a stranger's likeness. A Fan Club may be needed for that creator's deeper features; it is not the same purchase as Premium for platform or custom companions.

Lovescape's [published boundaries](https://lovescape.com/blog/what-is-lovescape-how-it-works-what-it-isnt-and-what-it-will-never-allow/) instead emphasize fictional adults and prohibit real-person likenesses, deepfakes, non-consensual material, and photo uploads. This makes Lovescape a poor fit if your actual goal is a creator-linked feed, and it makes Swipey's creator category a poor substitute for Lovescape's fictional-character-first brief. Keep every test character clearly adult and fictional; never use another person's face or identity as a shortcut.

## Treat the virtual currencies as different products

Lovescape [says](https://help.lovescape.com/hc/en-us/articles/30828415556881-Lovescape-Chips-What-They-Are-How-They-Work-and-How-to-Use-Them) its Chips are chiefly used for image generation. The per-image cost is shown before generation; purchased Chips do not expire but are non-refundable, and an active subscription is required to buy them. Promotional Chips may follow separate campaign rules. Check the current plan and token history rather than treating “monthly price” as the total media budget.

Swipey's [Hearts FAQ](https://swipey.ai/faq/hearts-billing/do-hearts-expire) says purchased Hearts remain while the account is active and are not dependent on an active Premium plan. They can fund extras such as calls billed by the minute, extended video scenes, and creator media. Premium still unlocks access to features; for example, its [voice-call FAQ](https://swipey.ai/faq/chat-relationship/what-is-the-call-memory-card-after-a-voice-call) says calls require Premium *and* spend Hearts per minute. A creator Fan Club is yet another choice. List the exact actions you want, then estimate the relevant plan, Hearts, and Fan Club costs separately. Neither provider's currency balance should be equated with a refund right.

## Compare continuity and privacy with one practical test

Write a brief for a fictional adult character: one motive, one harmless remembered fact, and a recognizable visual trait. On Lovescape, build that character and chat across a topic change; generate two safe, different scenes and check whether identity stays coherent. On Swipey, test one Platform Model and, if Premium is acceptable, a Custom AI built from the same brief. Do not present a Verified Creator Model as an identical input: it is tied to an actual creator's identity and content. Score continuity, clarity of controls, image consistency, and every paywall encountered. Those are suggested evaluation criteria, not measured results from this publication.

Privacy also differs in what an action leaves behind. Swipey's documented Call Memory card saves duration, the full transcript, and media sent during a voice call in chat history. Before calling, check that this persistence is acceptable and learn the current deletion process. For either service, read its live retention, visibility, and account-deletion controls before putting personal details in a conversation. Lovescape's no-photo-upload rule means the test should use prompts and platform-generated images, not source photos.

## Final verdict

Choose Lovescape when the main task is developing one fictional adult companion across conversation and generated images, and its separate Chip costs fit your expected use. Choose Swipey AI when browsing a feed, relationship progression, voice, or a verified creator's authorized experience is the actual reason you would return. Neither is a universal winner: compare the specific companion type, paid layers, data retention, and output you need before subscribing.
