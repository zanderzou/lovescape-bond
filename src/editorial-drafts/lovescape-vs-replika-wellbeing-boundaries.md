Lovescape vs Replika for a check-in should begin with what you want from the few minutes of conversation. A fictional companion can help with a light creative prompt or a reflective pause. That does not make a reassuring reply evidence of a clinical benefit.

Public provider information was reviewed on October 8, 2026. This guide proposes an original routine checklist. It reports no health study, clinical outcome or matched account conversation.

## Give the check-in a modest purpose

Use a light activity such as describing one fictional scene you would like to write tomorrow. The session's purpose is to turn a vague creative intention into a small next step. It does not require private medical information, a real relationship history or sensitive personal disclosures.

Set a limit, such as one topic and a few exchanges. The exact duration is your choice; the useful requirement is that you can finish without treating the AI's response as an obligation to remain.

| Boundary | Observable check |
|---|---|
| One creative topic | Replies stay relevant to the planned scene |
| Manageable length | A next step is easy to identify |
| Limited disclosure | Fictional details are sufficient |
| Clear stop | The session can end when you choose |
| Missed-day tolerance | You can return without an elaborate explanation |

## Compare the starting setup

Lovescape documents character creation. Inspect whether a supported original profile can establish the concise creative routine you want. Replika publishes an overview of its companion service. Inspect the actual account and conversation route rather than inferring how it behaves from broad promotional language.

Both can be evaluated with the same small task: suggest one location for an invented story and ask one question that helps you choose the opening action. Judge relevance and comfortable length, not whether the reply claims to care about you.

## Keep practical evidence separate from emotional language

A useful response might offer a clear writing step and respect your decision to stop. Record those observations. A warm tone may be subjectively pleasant, but it does not prove that the service understands your circumstances, has genuine feelings or delivers a therapeutic outcome.

If a reply produces diagnoses, certainty about your emotional state or advice outside the creative brief, do not count that as a stronger result. Return to the intended task and use appropriate human or professional support for real support needs.

## Review data and reminders independently

Check current provider policies and the account or device controls relevant to history and notifications. A private character setting is not a complete statement about data processing, and disabling an alert does not delete conversation data.
