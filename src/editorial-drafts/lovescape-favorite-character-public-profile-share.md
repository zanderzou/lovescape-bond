Treat a favorite marker and public sharing as separate controls. Lovescape's profile help describes the heart icon as a way to reach frequently used characters, while sharing a profile link depends on the character's public visibility.

Do not infer one state from the other simply because both actions appear near the same character image.

## Use a favorite for your own navigation

The documented heart control adds a character to favorites; selecting it again removes the favorite. That gives you a simple way to find a frequently visited character without changing its description.

## Inspect visibility before sending a link

For an original adult travel-guide character, you might want quick personal access while still refining the introduction. That personal preference is not a reason to publish an unfinished profile.

Open the relevant character profile and inspect its current visibility setting where available. Lovescape's help distinguishes private characters from public ones and describes copying a public profile link to share it.

If you intend to share, review the bio, image and other visible details first. Keep the content fictional and remove anything you did not mean to place in a public description.

## Do not use a link as a privacy test

Copying a URL alone does not explain what another account can view. Likewise, removing a favorite is not a substitute for changing a public profile's visibility.

If the current interface does not clearly show the state you need, consult the current profile controls or support before changing a broader setting just to experiment.

The provider's general privacy descriptions should not be expanded into a guarantee that every copy, shared screenshot or external recipient is under your control. Inspect the specific profile and action instead.

This guide does not alter an account or send a link. Its practical distinction is straightforward: use a favorite to organize your own navigation, and make a separate deliberate decision about public visibility and sharing.
