Lovescape's chip documentation distinguishes recurring subscription chips from purchased top-ups and says unused subscription chips expire at renewal. If your project spans the renewal date, identify which image actually needs to be ready first.

## Put the required asset before the optional work

A two-part adult fictional detective story needs a desk scene this week and a train platform scene next week. Read the current balance, renewal date, and quote for the desk image. Record the value under the balance type you can see. Leave the optional train image in your outline until you can see the new balance and its own quote.

![Read the plan date. Distinguish subscription and top-up chips. Decide which image you need now.](/assets/editorial-lovescape-chip-expiration-next-session-plan.png)

## Track the next decision by date

Use the plan and activity details on your own account. Keep purchased chip records separately from the included amount. If you are unsure which pool a charge used, use the timestamp and action details to ask for an explanation before repeating the generation.
