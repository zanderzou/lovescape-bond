Lovescape describes subscription chips and separate top-ups. Its help says an image's chip cost is shown before generation. Read the quote for the actual operation before assuming a Premium entitlement covers it without a balance change.

## Match the quote to an actual image

Suppose you want a single illustration of an adult fictional station announcer at a desk. Open the generation screen, choose the required options, and read the charge shown. If you also plan a second image later, keep its expected allocation separate. Confirm the active plan and the kind of chips available to use.

![Confirm the signed-in plan. Read the image's displayed quote. Compare with the available pool.](/assets/editorial-lovescape-premium-one-image-cost-quote.png)

## Review the decision when settings change

A higher-cost setting or a later attempt may produce a different quote. Read it before confirming each action. Preserve the first screen's amount in your private notes if you need to ask about a balance discrepancy. Compare the displayed plan inclusions, rather than using a generic allowance as the only estimate.
